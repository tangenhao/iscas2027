\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presents Q-DARE, an algorithm--hardware co-design for efficient video diffusion inference. QAC addresses the decision-domain mismatch introduced by naively combining timestep caching with low-precision execution, aligning cache decisions with the quantized trajectory. DARE addresses the matrix-axis asymmetry of spatial/temporal attention through output-lane adaptation for $QK^{\mathsf T}$ and boundary-aware segmented reduction for $PV$ on a shared PEA. Across three Open-Sora v1.2 workloads, the equal-peak ideal-fused cycle projection achieves \QDAREFusedGeoA$\times$ geomean STDiT speedup over measured A100 and \QDAREFusedGeoViDA$\times$ over the same-A100-bridged ViDA estimate. The full-overlap upper bounds are \QDAREUpperGeoA$\times$ and \QDAREUpperGeoViDA$\times$, respectively. These results show that numerical-domain alignment and dimension-adaptive execution are complementary for efficient low-precision video diffusion inference.
