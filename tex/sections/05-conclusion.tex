\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Q-DARE co-designs cache decisions and attention execution for low-precision video diffusion. QAC aligns reuse decisions with the W8A8/INT8 trajectory, while DARE reuses one PEA for output-axis adaptation in $QK^{\mathsf T}$ and reduction-axis adaptation in $PV$. On Open-Sora v1.2, Q-DARE reaches 76.61 VBench versus 76.77 for FP16, delivers \QDAREStepGeoA$\times$ geomean STDiT step-throughput and \QDAREEnergyGeoA$\times$ energy-efficiency gains over A100, and achieves \QDAREViDAThroughputGeo$\times$ geomean video throughput with \QDAREViDAreaGeo$\times$ area efficiency over ViDA.
