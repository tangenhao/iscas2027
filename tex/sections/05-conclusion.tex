\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presents Q-DARE, an algorithm--hardware co-design for efficient video diffusion inference. QAC addresses the decision-domain mismatch introduced by naively combining timestep caching with low-precision execution, aligning cache decisions with the quantized trajectory. DARE addresses the matrix-axis asymmetry of spatial/temporal attention through output-lane adaptation for $QK^{\mathsf T}$ and boundary-aware segmented reduction for $PV$ on a shared PEA. Across three Open-Sora v1.2 workloads, Q-DARE achieves \QDAREGeoA$\times$ geomean end-to-end speedup over A100 GPU, while reaching \QDAREViDAThroughputGeo$\times$ the geomean video throughput and \QDAREViDAreaGeo$\times$ the geomean area efficiency of ViDA.
