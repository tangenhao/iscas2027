\section{Quantization-Aligned Caching}
\label{sec:qac}

\subsection{Decision-Domain Mismatch}
Let $F_t$ denote the low-cost feature used by the timestep-cache policy before executing the expensive STDiT at denoising step $t$. Conventional caching measures its normalized full-precision (FP) variation,
\begin{equation}
 d_t^{\mathrm{FP}}=
 \frac{\lVert F_t-F_{t-1}\rVert_1}
      {\lVert F_{t-1}\rVert_1+\epsilon}.
 \label{eq:fp_indicator}
\end{equation}
The predictor is calibrated under FP execution, whereas the actual recompute path follows fixed W8A8/INT8 execution. Rounding, clipping, and piecewise-constant quantization regions can suppress a visible FP change or make a small change cross a quantization boundary. Hence the ordering induced by $d_t^{\mathrm{FP}}$ need not match recomputation importance on the quantized trajectory. We refer to this misalignment as the \emph{decision-domain mismatch}.

\subsection{Quantization-Aligned Indicator}
QAC evaluates cache relevance in the same numerical domain as the recompute path. With frozen quantizer scale $s$, it forms
\begin{equation}
 \widetilde F_t=D(Q_s(F_t)),\qquad
 d_t^{\mathrm{Q}}=
 \frac{\lVert \widetilde F_t-\widetilde F_{t-1}\rVert_1}
      {\lVert \widetilde F_{t-1}\rVert_1+\epsilon},
 \label{eq:q_indicator}
\end{equation}
where $Q_s(\cdot)$ and $D(\cdot)$ denote quantization and dequantization. The same calibrated quantizer is therefore used both to execute the model and to decide whether execution is needed. QAC introduces neither a separate precision mode nor an auxiliary correction network: every recomputed GEMM and attention operator remains on the fixed W8A8/INT8 datapath.

\subsection{Calibration and Runtime Policy}
Offline calibration pairs $d_t^{\mathrm{Q}}$ with the observed output change of the quantized model and fits a lightweight timestep-aware mapping
\begin{equation}
 \widehat r_t^{\mathrm{Q}}=g_q(d_t^{\mathrm{Q}},t).
 \label{eq:qac_mapping}
\end{equation}
At runtime, QAC computes $d_t^{\mathrm{Q}}$ before STDiT execution, evaluates $g_q$, and feeds the predicted change to the baseline thresholded cache rule. Cached states are reused while the accumulated predicted variation remains within the reuse budget; recomputation refreshes the cached state when the budget is exceeded.
